## Full description

Manage everyday payments from one place with MafitaPay.

• **NGN wallet** — view your balance and transaction activity.
• **Transfers** — send money to supported bank accounts and MafitaPay users.
• **Wallet funding** — use the deposit methods available to your account.
• **Airtime, data and bills** — buy supported mobile top-ups and pay supported utilities.
• **Crypto** — buy supported assets for an external wallet, or use supported crypto deposit features where available.
• **Transaction history** — review transaction status, details and receipts.
• **Account protection** — use your transaction PIN and available device or passkey security features.

Feature availability, supported assets, payment methods, limits, rates and fees may vary by account eligibility, provider, network and location. Crypto prices can change quickly and crypto assets can lose value. Check the asset, network, amount, recipient and fees before confirming. Blockchain transfers may be irreversible. MafitaPay does not provide investment advice.
